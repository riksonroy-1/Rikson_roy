THANKANS THE CARD PARTY by Rikson Roy

The ultimate card party with a local twist.

THANKANS: THE CARD PARTY is a vibrant, color-matching, UNO-style card game. Empty your hand first to win, challenge smart AI opponents offline, connect via local hotspot, or join friends online. Climb the ranks and remember to call BOOM!

Multiplayer modes Play offline, locally via hotspot, or join online rooms.
Smart AI Bots Challenge Thankan Ace, Blitz, and others in offline matches.
Trophy ranking Earn trophies for winning and climb from Bronze to Champion.
BOOM mechanic Tap BOOM before the timer runs out when holding 1 card!

THANKANS POOJYAM VETTU by Rikson Roy

A Dots and Boxes style game with local character.

THANKANS POOJYAM VETTU is made for entertainment and built around the familiar Poojyam Vettu / Dots and Boxes idea. It includes offline play, online room-based multiplayer, player progress, game settings, sound controls, themes, a store, and coin rewards.

Offline mode Play locally and challenge the Thankan-style game opponent.
Online rooms Create and join rooms with codes for friend matches.
Progress system Tracks wins and completion so players can keep improving.
Store and themes Includes coins, unlockable board themes, and sound settings.

THANKANS SOS by Rikson Roy

SOS with a twist of Malayalam humour.

THANKANS SOS is the classic SOS board game reimagined with Malayalam humour and playful Thankan-style character. Challenge friends, fill the grid with S and O, and enjoy hilarious commentary that makes every match entertaining.

Malayalam humour Hilarious local comedy woven into every match and moment.
Classic SOS gameplay The timeless SOS board game with a fresh, fun interface.
Multiplayer fun Play with friends locally and compete for the highest score.
