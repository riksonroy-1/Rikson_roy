Game Description

Thankans: The Card Party is a vibrant, color-matching, UNO-style card game where players compete to empty their hands first. The game includes:

  • Offline Bot Battles — Play against smart AI opponents (Thankan Ace, Blitz, Cipher, Drift, Echo, and Flux).
  • Local Hotspot Multiplayer — Play with friends in the same room using Wi-Fi / Hotspot discovery without needing internet.
  • Online Multiplayer — Create or join rooms across the internet in real-time.
  • Ranked Trophies — Earn trophies for winning matches to climb the ranks from Bronze to Champion.
  • BOOM System — Players must call "BOOM" when holding a single card, or face a draw penalty!
ℹ️ Entertainment Purpose

Thankans: The Card Party is made purely for fun and entertainment. The AI bots are fictional opponents designed to provide an engaging offline or mixed-multiplayer experience.
